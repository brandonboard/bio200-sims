Tips for students

  • Your work is saved in the browser you use. Finish a lab on the same device, and don't use a private or incognito window.
  • When a lab asks for a report, use its Copy report button and paste the text into your Canvas assignment.
  • Larger screens give the most room, but every lab also works on a phone.
